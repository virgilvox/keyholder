The GLB models used by the game and the kit's colour map, as distributed (Models/GLB format).
tools/prep-cars.mjs converts them into assets/models/cars.glb: vertex colours sampled from the
colour map, low poly wheels, scaled to real car lengths, turned so the length runs along +x.
