VRoid Project sample avatars released under CC0 (copyright waived). Downloaded 2026-11-10 from the
OpenGameArt collection "VRoid Studio CC0 models" (https://opengameart.org/content/vroid-studio-cc0-models),
which mirrors pixiv's VRoid help article on the sample models' conditions of use
(https://vroid.pixiv.help/hc/en-us/articles/4402614652569). Each file's own VRM meta allows
modification and redistribution (hub.vroid.com licence link: redistribution=allow, modification=allow,
credit=unnecessary; Base_Male, Base_Female and HairSample_Male also declare licenseName CC0).

The copies here were shrunk by tools/slim-vrm.mjs (images over 1024 px scaled down and stored as WebP;
meshes, skins, morphs and the VRM extension unchanged). tools/prep-vrm.mjs bakes them into
assets/models/char_{h,m,o,p,u,k,e,v,g,w,r}.glb.

The v1.2 bodies (char_a, char_i ... char_p2 in tools/prep-vrm.mjs CAST) are all made from the samples in
this folder and in source/vrm: another sample's hair or whole head grafted on, garments dropped and grown
back from the body skin as painted shells, recolours. Nothing else is in them: the shell textures are
drawn by the bake.

Looked at and not used:
- VIPE Heroes (Polygonal Mind; listed as CC-BY by the Open Source Avatars registry,
  github.com/ToxSam/open-source-avatars). Anime styled and varied, but each file's VRM meta says
  licenseName CC_BY together with allowedUserName ExplicitlyLicensedPerson, and no VIPE page states the
  terms. Unclear, so skipped.
- 100Avatars rounds 1 to 3 (Polygonal Mind, CC0). Clear licence, but nearly all are mascots and the few
  people are a chunky low poly style that does not sit with the VRoid cast.
- Quaternius character packs (CC0). Fine to use, but a different rig and a realistic low poly look; the
  VRoid kitbashes covered the need without a second pipeline.
- The VRoid sample clothing meshes and textures mirrored in github.com/madjin/vrm-samples: no licence of
  their own stated there, so skipped.
